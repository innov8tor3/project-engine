Why community immunity matters

A loss of “Fun” isn’t just aesthetic — it’s metabolic. If Level 4 feels like a chore, the community will not inhabit it.

project-engine currently has sprawl. In the transition to a functional Trust & Contribution Rubric, that sprawl becomes a map of contribution pathways — not noise to be deleted, but signals to be followed.

“Security without play produces compliance. Play without structure produces entropy. The Sovereign Immune System holds both.”

This rubric is the starting point and perpetual North Star for building digital assets that stay aligned with the GamerPrise ethos of Fun & Depth.

Onboarding pathway

From first commit to sovereign contributor. Stay on task. Stay in community.

1

Join the signal

Star / fork innov8tor3/project-engine. Open an issue or PR that already shows a hint of play.

2

Self-score against the rubric

Use the scorer above. Aim for ≥7.0. Document your reasoning in the PR description — that is evidence of play.

3

Leave stigmergic traces

Screenshots, short Loom clips, or annotated diffs become the next person’s map. Sprawl turns into pathways.

4

Delegate & level up

Once an asset can be managed without direct intervention, you unlock deeper sovereign roles inside GamerPrise Level 4.

🗺️

Stigmergic traces

After members pass the rubric gate, they gain access to illustrative screenshots and contribution patterns left by previous players. Follow the pheromone trail — don’t rebuild the maze.
